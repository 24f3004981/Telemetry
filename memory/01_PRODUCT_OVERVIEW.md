# 01 — Product Overview & Positioning

## 1. The Core Problem
In enterprise software sales—specifically for modern data platforms like **IOMETE** (lakehouse on customer Kubernetes and cloud storage)—the critical bottleneck in closing deals is rarely technical curiosity. The friction is:
> **"I have to justify this purchase to my boss."**

Internal champions and sales engineering teams spend weeks compiling spreadsheets, drafting slide decks, calculating cloud egress/storage tradeoffs, answering security questionnaires, and convincing the CFO, CIO, and CISO that replacing or supplementing Snowflake/Databricks/legacy Hadoop will save money and ensure data sovereignty.

## 2. The Solution: Telemetry (IOMETE Boardroom)
Telemetry is an executive decision engine that compresses this 3-week justification process into a **3-minute interactive boardroom experience**.

It takes prospect context (voice, notes, or structured input) and deterministically produces:
1. **Targeted Deployment Architecture**: Customer Cloud Kubernetes, On-Premises, Air-Gapped, or Hybrid.
2. **Transparent 3-Year TCO & ROI Model**: Side-by-side cost comparisons, projected annual savings, payback timeline, and infrastructure compute sizing.
3. **Sovereignty & Governance Assessment**: Clear score (0–100) and rationale evaluating data residency, audit logging, RBAC, and object storage ownership.
4. **1-Page C-Suite Approval Memo**: Print-ready, executive-formatted justification document for the CFO/CIO.
5. **60-Second Audio Briefing**: Spoken executive summary rendered via ElevenLabs TTS in a calm, confident board-level tone.

## 3. Target User Personas
- **IOMETE Sales Representatives & Account Executives**: Need a quick way to generate credible board-ready proposals during or immediately after discovery calls.
- **IOMETE Solutions Engineers (SEs)**: Need defensible compute sizing, transparent assumption modeling, and audit trails.
- **Enterprise Internal Champions** (VP of Data, Data Platform Lead, Chief Architect): Need air-tight financial and security arguments to take to internal procurement and the board.

## 4. Stakeholder Value Proposition
| Executive Stakeholder | Primary Question Addressed | Telemetry Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CFO** | "Why are we spending this, and what is the 3-year net financial impact?" | 3-Year TCO model, transparent unit economics, projected ROI, payback period in months. |
| **CIO** | "How does this fit our cloud strategy and operational scalability?" | Architecture diagram, Kubernetes compute footprint, workload sizing, cloud credit reuse. |
| **CISO** | "Where does our data live, who has keys, and does it satisfy compliance?" | Sovereignty & Governance assessment, customer VPC retention proof, RBAC & audit trail posture. |
| **Head of Data** | "Will this handle our complex analytics and user concurrency?" | Workload profiling, vCPU allocation model, predictable licensing without per-seat tax. |
